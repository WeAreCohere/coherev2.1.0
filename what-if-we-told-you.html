Agency over aid

Aid can provide vital resources and support, but it should not come at the expense of people's ability to shape the decisions that affect their lives and futures.

Agency means having the power to define priorities, make decisions and lead on solutions, and humanitarianism should be designed to support that power, rather than replace it.

“We are the experts of our own lives.”

Sandra Ndayishimiye, WOPEDE
